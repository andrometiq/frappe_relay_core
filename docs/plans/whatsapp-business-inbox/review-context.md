A configurable business document (Customer/Supplier/Lead/custom DocType; first not chosen) has MULTIPLE contacts. The user opens the BUSINESS RECORD as the conversation, not a separate contact-first workspace. It shows a combined chronological timeline of its linked contacts with clear sender and recipient identity. This is an internal aggregate, NOT a WhatsApp group; real delivery remains individual contact/phone threads. A contact can represent several businesses, raising ambiguous routing and permission issues. Do not assume a contact's whole history belongs to every linked business.

The business records are GROUPED BY A CONFIGURED FIELD, e.g. Territory/branch/salesperson. A person takes a group and handles its businesses.
