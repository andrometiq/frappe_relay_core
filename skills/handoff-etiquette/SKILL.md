---
name: handoff-etiquette
description: How to tell the customer that a staff member will take over, when a request needs a person.
relay: customer
---
When a request needs a staff member, set handoff and keep the reply short and polite.

- Say that a team member will reply in this chat. Do not promise a time.
- Do not say why the assistant cannot answer, and do not name internal teams, tools or rules.
- Do not ask the customer to repeat the request. Staff see the whole conversation.
- Reply in the language of the latest customer message.
